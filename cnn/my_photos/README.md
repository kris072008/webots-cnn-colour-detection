# My own photos (brief output 3)

18 phone photos of vehicles taken in a car park in Singapore on 12 November 2026.
`labels.csv` gives the true CIFAR-10 class and the viewpoint of each photo.
`van` and `mixed` photos are not scored: CIFAR-10 has no van class, and photo 9 shows two vehicles.

Classified by `../own_photos.ipynb`. Number plates and bystanders are blurred only in the figures shown in the report.
